# Commercial licensing

Terminals is **dual-licensed.** Pick the lane that fits you.

## Lane 1: open source (free)

- The **engine** (`mcp/`, executables) is **AGPL-3.0-or-later**.
- The **words** (commands, skills, agents, docs, READMEs) are **CC BY 4.0**.

This is free for everyone: individuals, teams, companies, research. Use it, fork
it, build on it. Two obligations only:

- **AGPL:** if you modify the engine and let others use it **over a network**
  (a hosted service, an API, a SaaS), you must offer those users the **complete
  corresponding source** of your modified version, under AGPL. Internal use and
  unmodified use have no such trigger.
- **CC BY:** keep the **attribution** to Intuition Labs LLC on the words you reuse.

If those terms work for you, you need nothing else. Welcome.

## Lane 2: commercial license (paid)

AGPL's network-copyleft is deliberately strong, and many companies' policies
forbid shipping AGPL code in a proprietary product or closed service. If that's
you (you want to **embed the Terminals engine in a closed-source or hosted
product without the AGPL source-disclosure obligation**), Intuition Labs LLC
offers a **commercial license** that lifts the copyleft requirement, with optional
support and an indemnity.

This is the same code. You are paying for the **right to keep your derivative
closed**. The software itself is identical.

**Who needs it:** SaaS/platform vendors embedding the engine in a non-open
offering; enterprises whose policy bans AGPL in shipped products; anyone who wants
written support or warranty terms.

**Who doesn't:** end users, open-source projects (AGPL-compatible), internal
tooling, research, evaluation.
